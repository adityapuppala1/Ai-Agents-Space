## Teams, handoffs and office moments — 11 September 2026

The office exists to show agents working the way people see a team work: who is on the job, who hands what to whom, and who is waiting. A multi-step job had no way to do that. Steps did not pass work to each other, no handoff was ever recorded between two agents, and so the office had nothing true to animate. This pass builds the whole path, from deploying a team to seeing each handoff in the office. Every moment is started by a record, plays once, and says where it came from.

### Design decisions

- **One token source.** `styles/tokens.css` is loaded first and is the only file that defines colour, type, radius, elevation, spacing and motion tokens, with light, dark and `prefers-contrast: more` values and `color-scheme`. - **A type floor.** Six steps (12/13/14/16/20/24 px), a 14 px base and nothing below 12 px. 216 declarations under 12 px were raised; 16 uppercase-with-tracking label rules were returned to sentence case.
- **Explicit cascade.** `main.jsx` imports tokens → components → base → experience → pages → shell, in that order, once. Tailwind was removed: after the office toolbar rewrite nothing used a utility class.
- **Three shell breakpoints.** A labelled sidebar above 1180 px, an icon rail with tooltips from 681 to 1180 px, and a bottom bar at 680 px and below with four primary destinations and **More**. Short screens (≤ 820 px tall) tighten the sidebar so every entry fits at 1280×720.
